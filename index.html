Problems solved

Admin work is rarely hard once. It is expensive because it repeats.

Most small teams do not need another dashboard. They need routine tasks to move without constant attention: emails sorted, leads routed, reminders sent, status changes tracked, and client updates logged in the right place.

ClawWorks looks for the recurring handoffs that cost you time every week, then turns them into dependable workflows that match how your business already runs.

How it works

A practical build process, not a giant transformation project.

2

Design the handoff

We define triggers, approvals, routing rules, exception handling, and where each update belongs.

3

Build and test

We launch the workflow in a controlled way, test edge cases, and keep human review where it matters.

4

Document and improve

You get a simple operating guide, update notes, and a backlog of future workflow improvements.
